# Weak Topics

Answers that were wrong, unclear or off the point, kept so they get covered in depth. The
mentor uses open entries to choose what to teach, quiz and deep-dive next.

An entry closes only after you correctly answer a later question aimed at that specific gap.

Format, newest at the bottom:

```
- YYYY-MM-DD | <topic-slug> | <source: check, quiz file, lab or doubt> | what you said or did | the actual gap | status: open
```

When an entry closes, its status becomes `closed YYYY-MM-DD`.
